Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ORYX mods, paint kits and downloads.

What is ORYX Modding Studios?

ORYX is an independent MotoGP modding studio focused on creating paint kits, custom liveries, mods and tutorials.

Which games are supported?

Our projects are mainly focused on MotoGP titles. Compatibility information is always listed on each download page.

How do I install a mod?

Each project includes installation instructions. If you are new to modding, check our tutorials section.

Are ORYX mods free?

Most ORYX projects are created for the community and made available through our downloads section.

How can I report a bug?

Use the Report Bugs page and provide details about the issue, including the game version and installed mod.
